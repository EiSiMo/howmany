# Privacy policy

how many? counts objects in your photos entirely on your phone. It collects no data.

- **Photos** are analyzed on your phone and never leave it. The app has no internet
  permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
- **No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.**
- **Problem reports** are only sent if you choose to. After a crash, or from the about
  page, the app can prepare a mail to us with technical details: the app version, your
  phone's model and Android version, how the app ended, and the app's log. It holds no
  photos. Your mail app opens with the report, and nothing is sent unless you send that
  mail yourself. We use reports only to fix problems and delete them once they're fixed.
